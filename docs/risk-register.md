# Risk Register

| ID | Risk | Impact | Likelihood | Mitigation | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|---|
| R-001 | Legal requirements for VPN sales, YooKassa receipts, taxes, and buyer regions are unknown. | High | High | Sandbox only; no real sales; legal review before production. | Product owner | Open |
| R-002 | Subscription bearer token leaks through logs, traces, metrics, or reverse proxy access logs. | High | Medium | Dedicated hostname, path redaction, no-store, token hash storage, secret scans, rotation runbook. | Access/Platform | Open |
| R-003 | Duplicate webhook or Kafka event creates double entitlement or payment effects. | High | Medium | Durable idempotency keys, unique constraints, normalized inbox, transactional outbox, and duplicate/out-of-order tests. Stage 4 consumers must add their own inbox. | Billing/Subscription | Mitigated for Stage 3 producer |
| R-004 | Fake or ambiguous YooKassa notification activates access incorrectly. | High | Medium | Persist provider key before I/O, retry ambiguous create with the same key, then verify provider state and amount/currency/shop/metadata through authenticated GET. | Billing | Mitigated for sandbox |
| R-021 | YooKassa credential, confirmation URL, or full provider payload leaks through errors or logs. | High | Medium | Safe provider error classification, no URL-bearing wrapped errors, normalized webhook inbox, redaction tests, and forbidden-data review. | Billing/Platform | Mitigated for Stage 3 |
| R-022 | Forged unique YooKassa object IDs exhaust webhook inbox storage before asynchronous verification rejects them. | High | Medium | Sandbox binds ingress to loopback. Production edge must rate-limit and enforce the current official YooKassa source-IP list; body/field limits and authenticated provider GET remain mandatory. | Billing/Platform | Open production blocker |
| R-005 | Invalid Xray config replaces working config on a node. | High | Medium | Pinned Xray validation, atomic swap, fixed process execution, last-known-good rollback, and real-Xray tests. | Provisioning | Mitigated for local Stage 6 |
| R-006 | Cross-service database access creeps in as implementation grows. | Medium | Medium | Separate DB users, architecture tests, CI checks, code review, AGENTS rules. | All services | Open |
| R-007 | Shared `internal/platform` becomes a hidden domain model package. | Medium | Medium | ADR, package ownership rules, architecture tests forbidding domain types there. | Platform | Open |
| R-008 | Failover provisioning failure is hidden while primary access works. | Medium | Medium | Explicit `degraded` result and distinct retry/failure scope; dashboards and alert routing remain Stage 8. | Provisioning/Access | Partially mitigated |
| R-009 | Aggregated traffic stats accidentally expand into browsing history or destination logging. | High | Low | Explicit forbidden data list, privacy review, schema review, log/metric scans. | Platform/Node | Open |
| R-010 | Stage 0 contract skeleton is mistaken for final implemented API. | Medium | Medium | Mark skeletons as Stage 0; require stage-specific ADR and tests before implementation. | Architecture | Open |
| R-011 | Happ compatibility differs from Stage 0 assumptions. | Medium | Medium | Re-check docs and run compatibility/golden tests in Stage 5; supersede ADR if needed. | Access | Open |
| R-012 | Production VPS provider forbids VPN/proxy usage or changes AUP. | High | Medium | Provider AUP review and approved provider list before production node onboarding. | Operations | Open |
| R-013 | Admin CLI credentials are overpowered or unaudited. | High | Medium | RBAC deny-by-default, short-lived credentials, append-only audit, reason-required operations. | Admin/Ops | Open |
| R-014 | Retention policy conflicts with future legal duties. | Medium | Medium | Sandbox retention only; payment records not auto-deleted until legal requirements are known. | Product owner | Open |
| R-015 | Plaintext subscription URL is generated before async provisioning and cannot be safely delivered later. | High | Medium | Generate token only after `access.ready.v1` through synchronous one-time issue endpoint. | Access/Bot | Mitigated in ADR 0016 |
| R-016 | Provisioning cannot receive VLESS credential without leaking it through Kafka. | High | Medium | Kafka carries only IDs/revision; provisioning fetches material over mTLS from access-service; audit every fetch. | Access/Provisioning | Mitigated in ADR 0017 |
| R-017 | Revoke request has no trustworthy proof that credentials left every assigned node. | High | Medium | Store a complete assignment separately from usable endpoints; bind success to desired/allocation revisions, exact unique node-set proof, node tombstones, and desired-vs-actual reconciliation. | Access/Provisioning | Mitigated in ADR 0022/0024 |
| R-018 | OpenAPI or implementation treats internal order/payment/token APIs as public, enabling IDOR. | High | Medium | mTLS service identity, endpoint allowlists, user context in internal path, no user_id in public body. | Platform/Billing/Access | Mitigated in ADR 0015 |
| R-019 | Notification-service announces VPN readiness from entitlement activation before provisioning succeeds. | Medium | Medium | `subscription.activated` is entitlement-only; user-ready notification uses `access.ready.v1`. | Notification/Access | Mitigated in ADR 0014 |
| R-023 | Cross-topic lifecycle reordering provisions stale or already expired entitlement. | High | Medium | Producer-owned aggregate sequence in envelope, Access lifecycle cursor, retryable paused-partition gap handling, stale collision rejection, and PostgreSQL-time eligibility checks. | Subscription/Access | Mitigated in ADR 0022 |
| R-024 | Public rate-limit or idempotency races consume unrelated budgets or return nondeterministic 500 responses. | Medium | Medium | One Redis Lua decision for both dimensions and global idempotency-key advisory serialization with concurrency tests. | Access | Mitigated in ADR 0022 |
| R-020 | Refund of one payment incorrectly preserves or revokes access funded by other payments. | High | Medium | Link each period to source payment; recalculate entitlement; emit refund-gap revoke only when current access disappears; reactivate at a future paid boundary. | Billing/Subscription | Mitigated in ADR 0011/0020 |
| R-025 | A compromised provisioning identity can add arbitrary VPN clients to every node. | High | Medium | Private management network, TLS 1.3 mTLS allowlist, exact node SPIFFE pin, allowlisted desired fields, no shell input, and separate health identities. Certificate rotation/revocation remains Stage 8. | Provisioning/Node | Partially mitigated |
| R-026 | Node and control-plane state drift after crash, partial revoke, or manual change. | High | Medium | Revision tombstones, generation-fenced higher-revision recovery, local durable state, actual-state reads, leased fair reconciliation, and newer-revision refusal. | Provisioning/Node | Mitigated for local Stage 6; production operations remain open |
| R-027 | Provisioning outcomes reorder across topics or a canceled reload leaves Xray stopped. | High | Medium | One transactional outcome sequence/cursor across all result topics, publication barrier, gap deferral, and independent bounded Xray consistency context with last-known-good restoration. | Access/Provisioning/Node | Mitigated in ADR 0024 |
| R-028 | Telegram accepts a notification but its response is lost, causing a visible duplicate after retry. | Medium | Low | Durable business dedupe, job lease, stable delivery ID guard, bounded retries, and explicit at-least-once runbook; never claim exactly-once. | Notification/Telegram | Residual by API semantics |
| R-029 | Stolen or misissued administrator certificate performs privileged actions. | High | Medium | Exact admin SPIFFE URI, enabled-principal lookup, default-deny RBAC, reason/idempotency, owner execution, immutable audit, and revocation runbook. Production key custody remains Stage 8. | Admin/Security | Partially mitigated locally |
| R-030 | Admin or support output leaks subscription URL, VLESS UUID, Telegram chat ID, provider ID, or node management URL. | High | Low | Support-safe owner DTOs, recursive server/CLI forbidden-field guard, no cross-service SQL, output/security tests, and fixed read routes. | Admin/All owners | Mitigated in Stage 7 |
| R-031 | Notification ordering across topics sends an extension/readiness/payment message after revoke/refund. | High | Medium | Source cursors plus causal delivery stream/sequence, stream advisory lock, FIFO claim barrier, terminal preemption, exact current Subscription/Access checks, payment/refund barrier, and regression tests. | Notification/Access/Subscription/Billing | Mitigated in ADR 0025 |
| R-032 | Owner commits an administrator mutation but a lost response is recorded as terminal failure. | High | Medium | Fenced attempt lease, nonterminal `outcome_unknown`, definitive-vs-indeterminate owner classification, stable action/correlation/owner key replay, and append-only attempt history. | Admin/All owners | Mitigated in ADR 0026 |
